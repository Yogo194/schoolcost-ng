# PRD  -  SchoolCost NG

## Product Name
SchoolCost NG

## One-line Description
A platform that helps Nigerian students and parents search, understand, and compare the complete cost of attending tertiary institutions.

## Problem Statement
School fee information in Nigeria is often scattered, outdated, incomplete, and difficult to compare. Fees may vary by school, course, level, student type, and academic session, while extra charges such as acceptance fees, departmental fees, accommodation, ICT fees, and medical fees are often excluded from the headline amount. Students and parents therefore struggle to understand the true cost of attendance before making admission decisions.

## Target Users
- Prospective students comparing tertiary institutions in Nigeria
- Parents and guardians planning education costs
- Current students submitting updated fee information

## Core Features  -  V1
1. **Search schools and courses**
   - Search by school name, course, state, ownership type, or institution type.

2. **View detailed fee breakdowns**
   - Show fees by course, level, fresh or returning student, and academic session.
   - Separate compulsory, optional, one-time, recurring, and accommodation charges.

3. **Compare schools**
   - Compare up to three schools using first-year cost, returning-year cost, accommodation, estimated degree cost, session, and source.

4. **View verification details**
   - Show source type, academic session, verification status, confidence level, last-reviewed date, and supporting evidence.

5. **Submit updates and corrections**
   - Allow users to submit new fees, report incorrect information, suggest a school, and upload evidence.
   - All submissions require admin review before publication.

## User Stories
- As a student, I can search for a school or course so I can find relevant fee information.
- As a fresh student, I can see the full first-year cost, including one-time charges.
- As a returning student, I can view fees relevant to my level and academic session.
- As a parent, I can compare up to three schools so I can understand the cost difference.
- As a user, I can see the source and last-reviewed date so I can judge whether the information is trustworthy.
- As a current student, I can submit an updated fee with evidence.
- As an admin, I can approve, edit, reject, or request more evidence before publishing a submission.

## Out of Scope  -  V1
- School applications or admission processing
- Scholarships and student loans
- Hostel booking
- School rankings and reviews
- Primary and secondary school coverage
- AI chatbot or automatic fee extraction
- Fee alerts and notifications
- Mobile app
- Monetisation features

## Definition of Done
The MVP is complete when:
- Users can search for a Nigerian tertiary institution or course.
- Users can select course, level, student type, and academic session.
- Users can view a structured fee breakdown and estimated first-year total.
- Users can compare up to three schools.
- Every fee record shows its source, session, verification status, and last-reviewed date.
- Users can submit an update or correction with evidence.
- Admins can review and moderate submissions.
- The product works on desktop and mobile.
- The product is deployed with real data for at least 15 institutions.
